About MEI Group

Empowering people. Building skills. Creating opportunities. MEI Group is a professional services division of MEI Press Holdings Ltd., providing integrated solutions in international education, road safety, occupational safety and health, and professional driver recruitment.

Our Services Contact Us

Who We Are

MEI Group brings together professional services focused on four key areas: International Education, Road Safety, Occupational Safety & Health, and Driver Recruitment. Our objective is to provide practical, accessible and professionally managed services to individuals, students, professionals, employers and organisations.

  • International Education — study abroad consultancy, admissions and visa guidance
  • Road Safety — training and awareness programmes for schools, fleets and institutions
  • Occupational Safety & Health — workplace safety and OSHA 2007 compliance training
  • Driver Recruitment — placement and recruitment support for drivers and employers
