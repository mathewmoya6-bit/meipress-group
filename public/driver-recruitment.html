FAQ

Common Questions

How do employers recruit drivers through MEI Group?
Employers can submit an application through this page or contact MEI Group directly. Recruitment may include candidate screening, documentation verification and relevant assessments.
What driver license classes do you support?
We support a broad range of driver categories, including motorcycle, light vehicle, professional, truck, trailer, bus and industrial/agricultural categories. Requirements may vary depending on the employer and destination.
Do you place drivers internationally?
MEI Group supports local and international recruitment opportunities. International placement is subject to employer requirements, immigration rules, licensing requirements and applicable regulations in the destination country.
Can companies submit driver requirements?
Yes. Employers can select "Employer" in the application form and provide their company, location and driver requirements.
