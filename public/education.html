What We Offer

Building a Safety Culture on Every Road

MEI Group delivers professional road safety training designed for real-world impact. Whether you're a school looking to protect students, a transport company seeking to reduce accidents, or an institution needing fleet assessments—we have a program for you.

  • Safe to & from School – Primary & secondary programs teaching children road safety awareness.
  • Defensive Driving – Advanced driver training for corporate and professional drivers.
  • Driver Profiling – Comprehensive assessment & behavior analysis for fleet managers.
  • Fleet Safety Assessments – Full audit of your fleet operations, policies, and driver records.

Our Programs

Road Safety Training Modules

Four focused programs designed to address the most critical road safety needs.

🏫

Safe to & from School

Age-appropriate road safety education for primary and secondary school students.

  • Pedestrian safety & crossing rules
  • School bus safety procedures
  • Bicycle & motorcycle safety
  • Interactive workshops & simulations
🚗

Defensive Driving

Advanced driver training for corporate fleets, professional drivers, and organizations.

  • Hazard perception & reaction
  • Emergency maneuvering
  • Fatigue & distraction management
  • Vehicle control in adverse conditions
📊

Driver Profiling

Comprehensive assessment and behavior analysis for fleet management and recruitment.

  • Driving behavior evaluation
  • Risk assessment reports
  • License & record verification
  • Recommendations for improvement
🛣️

Fleet Safety Assessments

Full audit of your fleet operations to identify risks and improve safety performance.

  • Policy & procedure review
  • Driver records & compliance check
  • Vehicle maintenance & inspection
  • Actionable safety improvement plan
