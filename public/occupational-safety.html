Why it matters

Kenya's Occupational Safety and Health Act (OSHA 2007) places compliance obligations on every workplace. Beyond the legal requirement, trained staff reduce incident rates and respond faster when something does go wrong.

We tailor sessions to your industry — construction, manufacturing, transport, or office environments each have different risk profiles.

FAQ

Common Questions

What does Occupational Safety & Health training cover?
Three core courses: First Aid (emergency response and certified first aiders), Fire & Safety (fire risk assessment, drills, and extinguisher use), and OSH (OSHA 2007 compliance and workplace risk management).
Is this training legally required?
OSHA 2007 places safety obligations on employers in Kenya. Our courses help you meet those requirements with documented, certified training.
How many employees can attend a session?
Group sizes are flexible — tell us your headcount and we'll structure the session accordingly.
