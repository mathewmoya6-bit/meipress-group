FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our services.

How do I register for a training session?
Visit our Training Calendar, find a session that suits you, and click "Register". You'll be taken to the Training Registration page to complete your details.
Which countries do you support for international education?
We support applications to the USA, Canada, Australia, UK, Germany, and many other countries through our 50+ university partnerships.
Do you offer education loans or financial support?
Yes! We provide guidance on education loans with no cosigner required, scholarships, and financial planning through our partner institutions.
What does Occupational Safety & Health training cover?
Our OSH training covers First Aid, Fire & Safety, OSH (OSHA 2007 compliance), and Workplace Safety. Each course is certified and industry-recognized.
What road safety programs do you offer?
We offer Safe to & from School, Defensive Driving, Driver Profiling, and Fleet Safety Assessments. Sessions run monthly throughout the year.
How do I get my certificate after training?
After successfully completing your training and any required assessment, MEI Group issues a certificate of completion. Certificates are typically issued within 5–7 working days and can be collected in person or sent digitally.
Can I register multiple people from my company?
Yes! For corporate groups, please select "Corporate" as the participant type during registration, or contact us directly at info@meipressgroup.com for bulk enrollment and group discounts.
